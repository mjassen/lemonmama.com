Buzzed Sign Of Alcoholism




A friend of mine, Musty Mustard.

Musty Mustard shared with me.

This year, Musty again attended a couple of annual events out in the community.

And Musty distinctly remembers going buzzed to both of these same annual events the past two years running.

Whereas the few years before these two years, Musty doesn’t.

These are dry events.

And so my friend Musty Mustard asks me.

Is this a sign that I’ve become an alcoholic including in the past two years?

And I say to my friend Musty Mustard.

It could be Musty, it could be.
