Serve Blog Out Only On Sundays




Who says I have to subscribe to always-on philosophy?

who says always connected?

so no.

I can serve my blog out only on sundays.

via my onionshare web server. 

or via my torrent.

lol torrent. 

nice.

maybe I'll share my blog posts out via torrent on sunday.

and I'll share my colleague's blog posts out via torrent on tuesday.

the interesting thing is.

If someone wants to read what I'm writing.

then you can bet they'll download it when sunday comes around.


in other words.


not only is it more relaxed.


It's also sort of a bullshit test at the same time.

if folks don't want to read it, then they're not going to tune in, no matter what day of the week.

but if folks want to read my blog, then, they're going to mark their calendar for sunday, and then download and read my blog when that day comes around.



In conclusion, serve blog out only on Sundays.
