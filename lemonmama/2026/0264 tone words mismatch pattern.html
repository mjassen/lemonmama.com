Tone Words Mismatch Pattern



I have a business partner, Henniker Bandit, who habitually tone of voice, doesn't match content of words.

Example is, Henniker Bandit's tone is smiling joking happy.
At same time the topic content of conversation is actual professional work.
The topic is business projects.
Serious or non-joking topic.

And so.

I today realize this is the pattern.
So now I can work with this and point this out to Henniker.
And I can ask what Henniker means and to clarify Henniker's tone or words or both.

I suspect though.

Henniker does this on purpose as a stronghand tactic.
Whether consciously or not.
Because Henniker can claim meant the other thing (the words not the tone) if confronted.
Here Henniker waits for a business partner to agree "voluntarily" for the one thing (the tone not the words)

I reckon Henniker Bandit leverages trust, tests good faith, when doing this pattern.
What would change this?
I reckon that this communication tone-words-mismatch can continue as long as Henniker's boss doesn't mind it.

But folks.
This is earth, we are humans.
Henniker isn't the only person I've seen do this.
Actually humans are clever, many humans do this.
We humans like to quickly change the paradigm in our favor.
Even or especially if this is a bait-and-switch.
Even or especially if this is gaslighting.
Even or especially if this is bullshitting.
