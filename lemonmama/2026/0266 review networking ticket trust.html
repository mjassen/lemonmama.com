Review： Networking Ticket To Trust



Writing my response to this blog post here:

"Networking Is Your Ticket to Building Trust in the Job Search in 2026"  |  https://www.getstart-ed.com/post/networking-is-your-ticket-to-building-trust-in-the-job-search-in-2026



Note: I like this blog post and I pretty much agree with most/all of it.
One thing this essay exposes is that getting trust is important.
I've heard that before, but this brings it up again in a valid light.
Because trust in my experience is a fickle thing. It comes and goes in our relationships, sometimes suddenly.
And so how does one know the moment when there is trust? 
How does one know what degree of trust there is at any moment?
How does know the velocity of trust or the rate of change of trust?
How can we all benefit from degrees of trust in a relationship?
Is it possible to ethically leverage trust?
Is it possible to unethically leverage trust?
How does one ethically leverage trust in a way that both/all parties benefit?



In conclusion, I like this blog post about networking and building trust in a job search, and, I posited these further questions about same here above, for further meditation.
